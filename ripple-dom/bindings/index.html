Bindings

attr.bindValue and attr.bindChecked keep an input and a Var in sync, both directions: typing writes the signal, and writing the signal updates the field.

They take a Var specifically - a read-only Signal would have nothing to write back to, and that is a compile error.

Type in the field and the readout follows; press the button and the field follows. One line, both directions.

The manual pair

attr.value plus on.input is the long form, for when the two directions are not symmetric: validation, normalising, or refusing a value outright. This field accepts only integers:

Anything that does not parse is refused: the signal keeps its last good value.
